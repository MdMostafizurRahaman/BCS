# Additional online source investigation

Status: incomplete investigation, not a finished historical corpus.

## Concrete answer-key disagreement

The [Admissionwar 39th BCS answer summary](https://www.admissionwar.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/39th-BCS-Q.Bank-Admissionwar.pdf), English item 20, gives *Aristocracy* for government by the richest class. The [Medha 39th collection](https://medha.com.bd/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/39th-BCS-Preliminary-Question.pdf), item 30, instead gives *Plutocracy*. These collections cannot both be ingested as authoritative answer keys. The original paper/options and an independent dictionary reference must be reconciled. Question numbering is different between these summaries, so source item numbers cannot be treated as original printed numbers.

## Further source leads

- [Rawfur's Notes](https://sites.google.com/view/rawfurnotes/bcs/bcs-preli) links separate 39th and 42nd PDF files on Drive. These are leads, not authenticated scans or acquired corpus files.
- [OnlineBCS archive](https://onlinebcs.com/category/previous-questions-solution/previous-bcs-questions-solution/) links separate 39th and 42nd solution posts. The archive itself is not the underlying paper.
- [QBookBD BCS index](https://qbookbd.com/bcs) lists health examinations with 100-question entries. Such an entry must not automatically be represented as a complete 200-question general-plus-medical paper.
- [Live MCQ public channel](https://t.me/s/livemcqofficial?before=1149) points to 42nd medical-part content inside its app. This identifies a possible source; it does not grant access or reuse permission.
- [MedMCQA](https://github.com/medmcqa/medmcqa) was considered as a large medical-question resource. It is not a Bangladesh BCS historical corpus. Importing thousands of its entries would not establish BCS pattern alignment or satisfy the requested original question production. No wholesale import was performed.

## Production consequence

Quantity cannot replace provenance. The app still has 264 questions and zero authenticated complete historical papers. None of the new links has been promoted to an actual exam in the app. `screenRedundancy` now flags normalized stem repeats and numerical parameter variants; this is an automated screen, not proof of semantic uniqueness. All existing questions still lack the learning-objective records required for meaningful coverage measurement.
